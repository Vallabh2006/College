1. Compile: (Make sure you are in Huffman folder)
CLI: g++ -O2 source/compress.cpp -o compress && g++ -O2 source/extract.cpp -o extract

2. Compress:
CLI: ./compress <input_file> <output_file.huff>
Example: ./compress sample.txt sample.huff

3. Extract:
CLI: ./extract <compressed_file.huff> <output_file>
Example: ./extract sample.huff restored.txt
